# 🌌 Solaris

> A living social universe powered by AI.

## Vision

Solaris is an interactive web application that transforms real time conversations into an exploration of the Solar System.

Instead of joining a traditional chat room, users travel through a realistic 3D Solar System where every planet is represented by a unique AI personality.

Each planet behaves differently, remembers past interactions, reacts to the emotional tone of conversations, and offers a different social experience.

The application combines:

- Real-time communication
- Artificial Intelligence
- 3D exploration

to create an experience that feels alive rather than static.
