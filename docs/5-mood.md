# 🌡️ Mood System

The Mood System makes conversations affect the world.

The goal is to make the world feel alive and responsive.

---

## Two Mood Levels

### Conversation Mood

Represents the current atmosphere of one conversation.

Examples:

- Calm
- Positive
- Humorous
- Tense
- Heated
- Chaotic

### Planet Mood

Represents the overall state of a planet.

It is influenced by its active conversations and recent activity.
